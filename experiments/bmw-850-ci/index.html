BMW 850 Ci

A psychedelic car-show pilot that never became a series.

Open on YouTube ↗

A different kind of car film

Made with a friend in Kyiv, this was an attempt to make automotive content with a deliberately strange, caricatured flavour. It was a pilot, and it stayed a pilot.

Contributions

Commissioned by Vlad Svoboda. Structure, narrative and direction: Eugene Andrushenko. Automotive writing: Vlad Svoboda. Cast: Vlad Svoboda, Eugene Andrushenko and Paulina Nelen. Special thanks: Sasha Argudyaeva.

Looking back Intentions, outcomes, and what I learned.

The flavour didn’t quite hit the mark for me. The narrative provides momentum, but my automotive material slows it down. On the page, the technical detail seemed to work; performed on screen, it didn’t hold attention. I still find the result awkwardly funny. It taught me something about the difference between knowing a subject and making it interesting in another medium.
