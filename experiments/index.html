02 / Experiments

Things I tried.

A record of experiments across art, technology and storytelling. Some became finished works. Others taught me something and stopped there.

From the ledger

2016 — onwards
  1. 01 Belka An EEG-driven world became a challenge: can you stay calm while everyone watches? EEG / virtual reality 2016 ↗
  2. 02 Sack the Tyrant A political rally simulator about the fear of speaking up, made for Lampu in Bali. Participatory art / performance 2023 ↗
  3. 03 Greed is God An experiment with choice, documented at the Bombay Beach Biennale. Participatory art / film 2019 ↗
  4. 04 BMW 850 Ci A car-show pilot, a lot of caricature, and some writing that worked better on the page. Experimental film / collaboration 2020 ↗
  5. 05 The Ocean Within A film from the ocean’s perspective, and ten years of learning how to make it. Hybrid documentary / collaboration 2016–2026 ↗
  6. 06 FML48 Forty-eight hours to make a film. Then the much longer work of developing the next one. Film competition / development 2018–2019 ↗
  7. 07 Moscow Misfits The collective connecting an Arctic film, a filmmaking competition and immersive performances. Creative laboratory / production collective 2018–2019 / selected work ↗

Dates describe the work, rather than when this page was last updated.
