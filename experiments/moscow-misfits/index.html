Moscow Misfits

A group of friends making films, putting on experiments and trying to find people who wanted to make things with us.

A creative laboratory

Moscow Misfits was the name we gave our creative laboratory and production network. The core team was Justin, Ani, Michael and me. Our old website called it “Creative Lab // Production services,” which covered a fairly broad range of intentions.

The work brought us between films, immersive performance and other ways of telling stories. We were interested in what happened when an audience could participate, when a story moved between formats, or when you tried to see the world from a perspective other than your own. Sometimes that meant a camera. Sometimes it meant a very large production problem in an unfamiliar place.

The site listed London, Moscow, Los Angeles, New York and Shanghai. It also had a talent roster spanning directors, filmmakers and people working with interactive stories and immersive experiences. The films on their individual showreels were their work; the projects below are where the collective's own history becomes more concrete.

The film that brought us together

Aquatilis, now The Ocean Within, began with an idea about making a film from the ocean's perspective. I brought Alex Semenov and his underwater world into the conversation. Friends became collaborators, we started filming in 2016, and the production became an education in just how many different things making a film involves.

The film kept going long after those first shoots and the original collective. Its production history now has a page of its own.

Making room for other filmmakers

In July 2018, we organised FML48, a competition in which teams had forty-eight hours to make a short film from a shared brief. It developed into awards, talks, publishing and work with the winning team on a future project. Ani and I were involved in that development work as well as organising the competition.

FML48 and RTFM collects the films, the brief and what followed the production weekend.

Waldorf collaborations

We also worked with Sean Rogg on FUTURO-X at Wonderfruit in Thailand in December 2018, followed by FUTURO-A aboard a Lufthansa flight from Frankfurt to Austin in March 2019. One involved a festival audience of thousands; the other took place inside an aircraft. Both required a fairly unusual kind of production.

The FUTURO-X film credits Moscow Misfits with film production. Its making-of names me as executive producer and drone camera, Justin Thomson and Greg McLaren as production managers, and Ani Dzavaryan as production assistant. The FUTURO-A film credits me as executive producer and Justin as producer and camera.

Sean conceived and directed the works, with choreography by AΦE and costume and textile design by Elena Martin. The individual films carry the fuller credits. My earlier attempt to take the existing Chapter Three show internationally did not happen; I had no involvement in making its original production.
