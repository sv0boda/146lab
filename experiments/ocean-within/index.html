Documentary / collaboration / Film

Ocean Within

A documentary project that has occupied years of my life.

Between two worlds

The film brings observation of White Sea marine life together with a constructed story featuring biologist Alexander Semenov and his real-life partner as versions of themselves.

Underwater, Alexander knows how to look and wait. People are more difficult. When he falls in love, the separation between those worlds begins to break down: jellyfish drift between apartment towers and sea angels glide through metro stations.

A sustained collaboration developed through years of production and editing, the film moves between underwater observation and staged city scenes without marking where the real ends and the imagined begins.

Its story also appears in Moscow Born, an essay about making the film, displacement and the difficulty of belonging.
