Projects.

2016 — onwards

  2. 02 Sack the Tyrant A fake protest at Lampu, and the fear of speaking up. Participatory art / performance 2023 ↗
  3. 03 BMW 850 Ci A car-show pilot, a lot of caricature, and some writing that worked better on the page. Experimental film / collaboration 2020 ↗
  4. 04 Greed Is God Gitane Tower, eight fake Rolexes, and my first solo artwork. Participatory art / film 2019 ↗
  5. 05 Shocker A high score, an NFT idea, and a lesson in needing a crowd. Participatory art / performance c. 2022 ↗
  6. 06 Waldorf Project A cold showcase, a festival in Thailand and a performance over the Atlantic. Immersive theatre / production 2016–2019 ↗
  7. 07 The Ocean Within A film from the ocean’s perspective, and ten years of learning how to make it. Hybrid documentary / collaboration 2016–2026 ↗
  8. 08 FML48 A weekend of films, lucky-cat awards and a community built with almost no budget. Film competition / development 2018–2019 ↗
  9. 09 Moscow Misfits We built a team to bring productions into Russia. The business proved harder than the shoots. Production services / collective 2018–2019 / selected work ↗
