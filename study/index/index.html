Some places to begin

Each project tells the story of what happened. Under “Looking back,” I return to it: what worked, what didn’t, and what I think differently about now.

  1. 01Technology experimentBelkaIn 2016, I went to a hackathon to see if we could build a world out of someone else’s brain activity. A few months later, I found myself using it to wind up fintech people.Project / 2016↗
  2. 02WritingHow I met your dog.A mountain walk, an injured stray, and a responsibility I hadn’t planned on.Writing / 2023↗
  3. 03Art & performanceGreed Is GodA business centre in an outhouse. A job that hurts. A fake Rolex when you retire. My first solo artwork.Project / 2019↗
  4. 04FilmThe Ocean WithinIn 2016, some friends and I decided to make a film from the perspective of the ocean. I thought we would figure out how along the way. Ten years later, we have a film, and I have considerably more respect for that question.Project / 2016↗
  5. 05Book excerptA Sunny Day.Moscow, 2008. A sunny drive through a world where wealth and violence live side by side.Writing / 2023↗
  6. 06Acting & communityDiistvoWe started with an acting course for people who weren’t actors. It became a growing business, a community and probably the most meaningful work I’ve done. Then the war scattered us. We tried to put it back together elsewhere, but we couldn’t put it back together the way it had been.Project / 2021↗

The same work can lead in several directions.

Who’s actually in control?

I keep coming back to freedom, but it means different things in different rooms. Sometimes it is being able to speak. Sometimes it is noticing a rule I have been obeying without knowing it. Sometimes I am the one setting the rules.

Follow this route · 9 entries ↗

Can you feel what I feel?

A colour, a room, a story, a person paying attention. I am interested in how little it can take for an internal experience to reach someone else, and how much gets lost or changed on the way.

Follow this route · 6 entries ↗

What makes a place home?

I have left places, lost places and tried to make new ones. A home needs somewhere to sit and running water. It also needs people, a relationship with its surroundings, and some way of belonging that survives an interruption.

Follow this route · 5 entries ↗

What are we really chasing?

Money, a watch, a summit, approval. The prize is easy to name. The reason we want it is often much less obvious. I like putting those two things next to each other and watching the story change.

Follow this route · 9 entries ↗

What do we owe each other?

A lot of this work begins with other people. Making something together brings obligations that outlast the exciting idea: doing the work, giving credit, looking after someone, knowing when a relationship cannot carry on.

Follow this route · 7 entries ↗

What changes us?

I left finance without knowing what I would become. Getting sober, learning to act, making a home, making art: each changed what I could notice, what I wanted, or what I thought I was capable of. The work in this thread is also work that did something to me.

This is a work in progress. I’m still going through years of writing, photographs, footage and unfinished stories, bringing older work into view as I go. New work is happening too, and will find its way here.
