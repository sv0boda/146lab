03 / About

Hey, stranger.

My name is Vlad Svoboda. This is my laboratory on the World Wide Web.

I write, make things, and follow questions into unfamiliar territory. Those questions have taken me through finance, film, performance, technology and the ways people experience the world.

This site brings some of that work together. It is a record of experiments: finished, unfinished, collaborative, and occasionally unsuccessful. Writing is a core part of it. I am also working on a novel.

More recently, I have been exploring local AI and data sovereignty. The older work is here partly because looking back helps me understand what I want to build next.
