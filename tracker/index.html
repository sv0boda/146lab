KEEPING THE OPTIONS OPEN

A premiere is a sequence, not a reset.

An application does not consume a premiere. A public screening, online availability or confirmed screening hold can change the routes that remain.

IF TROMSØ SCREENS FIRST

Norway · January 2027

World, international and European premieres are used. VdR’s Burning Lights would normally fall away. A US premiere and other national-premiere opportunities may remain.

IF TRUE/FALSE SCREENS FIRST

USA · March 2027

With no earlier European screening, a European premiere may remain for VdR. The screening order and any VdR participation hold must be agreed before committing.

AFTER AN EARLY EUROPEAN LAUNCH

The circuit continues

German, Czech, Latvian, Icelandic or Georgian premiere routes can remain, depending on the section and earlier territories. Flexible festivals also offer later screenings.
