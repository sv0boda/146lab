Note

Happy New Year, folks.

2024-01-02 ~3 min Telegram

Happy New Year, folks.

In this short letter, I want to thank you.

When the war in Ukraine began, and I was on my way out of Kyiv, I made a strong resolution to write my story down and publish it. I dabbled across a variety of mediums and wrote down short, disjointed stories, all in the middle of my fourth refugee period. Last year, I finally settled in a somewhat stable place and started doing the work.

Eventually, I started this blog, and you started subscribing to it. Every now and then, one of you would give me a piece of feedback or tell me how my words touched you. At the end of the year, I even got my first paid subscribers. My heart melts when I think about it. Deep creative work is akin to sickness. One feels a need to express oneself, fighting the fear of digging out the long-suppressed traumatic experiences. And even though the experience of opening up and being vulnerable is something that only keeps on giving, it's a painful process nevertheless. Having you guys witness it, react to it and perhaps recognise something of yours in it is the greatest reward for me.

Don't get me wrong, I am not writing my story to be liked. I am writing it because I must, yet when it connects to you, it is a good indication that I managed to hit a truth. Some feeling or emotion that goes beyond me, something that exists in the back of our minds and is released through expression. That resonance is the greatest gift from the audience I can wish for and, at the same time, the greatest gift I can give.

And I thank you for that. I would not hope to have your attention otherwise.

The last few weeks were very hectic. My birthday in late November has set off a sequence of events that culminated in the unexpected birth of five puppies by the dog we rescued earlier last year. I have been struggling to get down to work, but now that new life conditions seem to have found a balance, I make a vow to be more disciplined and to deliver new chapters on a much more regular cadence of at least 2 a month.

The next one is to be published by the end of this week. In that one, I get out of chronological order and jump 15 years forward to April of 2023.

I hope you enjoy it.

With love and gratitude.

Vlad Svoboda

P.S.

I want to take a moment to express gratitude to those who are helping me make this book a reality.

My dear friend Lisa Gray, who helped me structure my ideas and formulate the skeleton of my book along with the general plan for its development and publishing, is supporting me regularly every step of the way.

My beloved fellow nerd, Dima Marusov, is helping me boil down each chapter, break down what's important and focus my writing while reminding me that my stories are worth telling.

My favourite person to argue with is Natasha Jacobs, who manages to correct a hundred mistakes in each draft after I do a pass myself and then another one with Grammarly.

And Justin Murphy, alongside his Indie Thinkers community, helped me understand the world of independent publishing and get into the habit of writing. If you want to start writing, check them out.
