Sack the Tyrant Fundraiser

2023-01-25 ~1 min Paragraph Mirror

Hey you.

My name is Vlad Svoboda. I am a film producer, a degen and an immersive artist. My favourite medium is interactive performances that push the boundaries of what can be done within an artistic experience. A few years back I made a work that was a commentary on the phenomenon of career building and workaholism. Recently I published a short film about it, you can check it out here:

Vlad Svoboda · YouTube · 6:44 Greed is God A short film documenting the earlier experiment with career building, workaholism and pain discussed in this fundraiser. Watch film ↗

At this moment I find myself in Bali, being displaced from Kyiv by a war. There is a local burn happening, organised by a Russian burners community in which I am revisiting the pain infliction performance technique.

This time it’s called “Sack the Tyrant”. I am planning to invite people into an enclosed immersive space where they’re going to find themselves in the middle of a big political rally. They will be wearing a shock collar and they will be encouraged to express themselves vocally. They will get electrocuted with increasing intensity for their expression and eventually when they decide they’ve had enough be extracted out of the rally into a police van. At that point they’ll get a physical object to commemorate their achievement of overcoming the pain and fear to express themselves as well as an NFT. Depending on the amount of shocks they can endure, they will have dropped a particular tyrant.

I am self funding this piece and I am asking for your support here. Please donate whatever amount you can to make this experience better. After the event I will make a film about it and publish it for all backers to see. I will include every address that donates in the credits no matter the size of donation.

Thank you.
